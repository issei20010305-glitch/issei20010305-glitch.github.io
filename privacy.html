Privacy Policy

Application: Daily Science Shorts
Operator: Issei (issei20010305@gmail.com)
Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who uses this application

Daily Science Shorts is a personal automation tool. Only its owner uses it, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and other people do not sign in to it.

2. Google user data we access

The application requests one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This lets it upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy or scheduling settings on the authorized YouTube channel. It does not read your email, contacts, files, watch history, comments, subscribers or analytics.

3. How the data is used

The OAuth credentials are used for one purpose: uploading videos that the application generated to the owner's channel. They are not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

The OAuth client ID, client secret and refresh token are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository. They are only available to the automated workflow that uploads the videos. They are never published, logged or put into the source code.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with any third party. Video content is sent only to YouTube, where it is published according to the owner's settings.

6. Third-party services

To produce the videos, the application uses Groq (script generation and speech-to-text) and Pexels (stock footage). No Google user data is sent to these services. YouTube's use of uploaded content is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

The credentials are kept only while the automation is running. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored secrets are deleted from the repository.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Daily Science Shorts' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
